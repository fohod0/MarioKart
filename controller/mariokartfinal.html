Drive equations

How the equations work
WForward input: 0 = not pressed, 1 = fully pressed
ALeft input, 0 to 1
SBack input, 0 to 1
DRight input, 0 to 1

Keys are always 0 or 1. Controller sticks/triggers and the on-screen sticks give anything in between (e.g. W = 0.5 at half throttle).

Left sets the left motor (ENA), Right sets the right motor (ENB). The answer means: +1 full forward, 0 stop, −1 full reverse. Anything above 1 or below −1 is capped.

Bluetooth: Chrome, Edge, Opera, or Brave (after enabling its Bluetooth flag) on a laptop. WiFi: any browser on any device - join the robot's WiFi network, then open http://192.168.4.1. Releasing all input, STOP, closing the tab, or losing the link all stop the motors.
